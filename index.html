about me

I am an emerging energy and sustainability professional working at the intersection of energy transition, industrial decarbonisation, and technology. My work explores how policy, technological change, and economic transformation are reshaping energy systems and the global transition to a low-carbon economy.

My background combines energy system modelling, policy analysis, and quantitative research. I have worked on China’s steel sector decarbonisation pathways, digitalisation and energy systems, renewable energy and green hydrogen, climate policy, and clean-technology supply chains. My experience spans Peking University, Sciences Po, the European Parliament, and the Research Council of Lithuania, giving me an international and cross-disciplinary perspective on complex energy and sustainability challenges. I use Python, SQL, Excel and Power BI to analyse data, develop research projects, and translate complex evidence into clear, policy-relevant insights.
